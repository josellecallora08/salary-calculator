Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

How this app uses storage

Payroll Lite PH is a client-only payroll calculator. It does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

Local browser storage

The app uses your browser’s local storage to keep the information needed for the calculator to work:

  • Your payroll profile and salary settings
  • Saved monthly payroll records
  • Your cookie-consent choice

This information stays in your browser and is not sent to a payroll server by the app.

Consent preference

The consent popup stores whether you selected Accept or Decline so it does not appear every time you open the app. You can remove this preference, along with all other locally stored payroll data, using the Delete local data button in the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app later adds analytics, login, or other services that use cookies or similar technologies, this policy and the consent choices will be updated before those features are enabled.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the person or organization that provided this Payroll Lite PH deployment.
