# Payroll Lite PH

Payroll Lite PH helps you estimate your Philippine semi-monthly salary. Set up your normal payroll details once, then use the calendar each month to record only absences and holiday work.

## Getting started

1. Open the payroll calculator.
2. Enter your daily salary.
3. Select your regular workdays.
4. Check your cutoff and payday settings.
5. Select **Save profile**.

Your profile will be remembered on the same browser and device.

## Calculating a month

1. Choose the payroll month.
2. In the calendar, click scheduled workdays that were unpaid. Selected dates are counted as unpaid absences.
3. Holiday dates are marked in the calendar with the holiday name.
   - Leave the holiday unchecked if you did not work.
   - Check the holiday if you worked that day.
4. Add other deductions only when needed.
5. Select **Calculate salary**.

The calculator automatically determines the workdays in each cutoff, holiday pay, statutory deductions, withholding tax, and both expected payouts.

## Understanding the results

The results show:

- Expected payout for the first payday.
- Expected payout for the second payday.
- Monthly take-home pay.
- Gross pay for each cutoff.
- SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax, and total deductions.

Select **View detailed breakdown** to see the calculation details.

## Contribution settings

SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax are estimated automatically from the saved salary and payroll rules. The advanced contribution fields are only for employer-specific adjustments.

Use **manual contribution amounts** only when you know your payroll uses different values.

## Saving and printing

- Select **Save month** to keep a local copy of a completed calculation.
- Use **Saved months** to open a previous calculation.
- Select **Print / Export PDF** to create a printable payroll summary.

## Important note

All results are estimates. Confirm holiday treatment, contribution amounts, withholding tax, employer policies, and final pay against your payslip or official payroll records. Your information is kept on the current device and browser.
