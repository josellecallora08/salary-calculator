Payroll Lite PH

Philippine salary calculator

Payroll Lite PH is a private Philippine payroll calculator for estimating daily-paid and monthly-paid salary. Set your salary basis, choose your work schedule, mark unpaid absences, and review detected holidays in a semi-monthly payroll estimate.

What the calculator estimates

  • Daily-paid and monthly-paid basic salary
  • First and second cutoff payouts
  • Unpaid absence deductions
  • Regular and special non-working holiday treatment
  • SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax estimates

Private and offline-friendly

Your payroll profile and saved months stay in your browser. The app can be installed on supported devices and used offline after it has been loaded once.

Common questions

Can I calculate monthly salary?

Yes. Select Monthly-paid during profile setup and enter the monthly basic salary. The calculator uses the configured monthly policy to estimate cutoff pay and unpaid absence deductions.

Are statutory deductions automatic?

SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, and withholding tax are automatically estimated using the bundled rules. Employer-specific settings and payslips should be used for final payroll decisions.

Does the calculator send my salary data to a server?

No. Payroll data is stored locally in this browser. Clearing browser storage or using the Delete local data button removes the saved information.
